Model a wall-mounted mini-split air conditioner indoor unit, glossy white plastic, approximately 0.90 m wide, 0.30 m tall, 0.22 m deep, mounted flat against a wall. The body is a rounded horizontal box: a large smooth front face panel framed by a thin raised border, gently curved bottom-front edge, and flat top and sides. Along the lower front curve run three long horizontal slots; each slot holds a slim airflow louver vane (dark recessed slot with a thin white vane blade). Articulations: (1) each of the three louver vanes is a revolute joint about a horizontal axis running along the unit's width (left-right), pivoting roughly -45 to +45 degrees to direct airflow up or down — model them as three independent revolute joints; (2) the large front face panel is a hinged service cover, revolute about a horizontal axis at its top edge, opening upward about 0 to 60 degrees to reveal the filter cavity behind it (model a shallow hollow recess and a thin removable-looking filter panel inside).
