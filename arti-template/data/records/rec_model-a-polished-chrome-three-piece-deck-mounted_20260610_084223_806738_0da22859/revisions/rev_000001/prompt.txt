Model a polished-chrome three-piece deck-mounted roman tub faucet, total spread about 0.30 m, in an Art-Deco style. Center piece: a tapered square-pyramid base (about 0.07 m square at the deck, narrowing upward, 0.08 m tall) with a stepped square cap, from which a wide flat-topped spout sweeps forward and curves down in a smooth waterfall arc, reach about 0.18 m; a small oval finial button sits on the cap. Left and right pieces: each is a matching smaller square tapered pyramid base (about 0.06 m square, 0.07 m tall) topped by a slim cylindrical stem carrying a four-spoke cross handle, spokes about 0.09 m tip-to-tip with small ball ends. All surfaces are mirror chrome on a dark deck. Articulation: the left cross handle is a revolute joint about its stem's vertical axis, range -180 to +180 degrees; the right cross handle is an identical independent revolute joint. The center finial button is a revolute diverter about the vertical axis, -90 to +90 degrees. Spout and bases are fixed. Reference image: picture/Other/Faucet/006.png
