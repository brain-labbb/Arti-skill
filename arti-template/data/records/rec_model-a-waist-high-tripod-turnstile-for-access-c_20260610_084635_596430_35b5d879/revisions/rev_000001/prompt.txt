Model a waist-high tripod turnstile for access control, about 1.0 m tall, 0.5 m wide, 0.28 m deep overall, with chrome arms extending roughly 0.5 m from the hub. The body is a slim brushed stainless-steel pedestal cabinet: a flat rectangular lower column (with a recessed rectangular access-panel outline and a small circular lock on its front face) that widens via a short chamfered transition into a deeper rectangular head box at the top. The head box has a dark grey/black speckled granite-look top plate slightly overhanging the steel housing, and a small black card-reader/indicator module on its left side near the top. From the front face of the chamfered transition, at about 0.95 m height, a polished chrome tripod rotor protrudes: a flat elliptical hub disc angled toward the user with three round chrome tube arms (about 40 mm diameter, 0.5 m long, capped ends) spaced 120 degrees apart around the hub axis. The rotor is the single articulation: a continuous revolute joint about the hub's own axis, which tilts roughly 45-55 degrees downward-forward from horizontal, letting the arm set index in 120-degree steps as a person pushes through.
