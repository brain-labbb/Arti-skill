Model a wall-mounted bathroom faucet in polished gold brass, about 0.30 m wide overall. Three units mount to a vertical wall plane: a central round escutcheon flange (about 0.07 m diameter) from which a cylindrical spout (about 0.03 m diameter) projects horizontally about 0.18 m before curving smoothly downward about 0.10 m to an open outlet; and two flanking valve assemblies, one on each side, each consisting of a stepped round escutcheon, a short cylindrical valve body, and a four-arm cross handle with slim cylindrical spokes (about 0.10 m tip-to-tip) and a knurled central hub. Everything is mirror-polished gold. Articulation: the left cross handle is a revolute joint rotating about the horizontal axis perpendicular to the wall (the axis the valve body projects along), range -180 to +180 degrees; the right cross handle is an identical independent revolute joint. The spout and escutcheons are fixed. Make the spout a hollow tube with a visible bore at the outlet. Reference image: picture/Other/Faucet/001.png
