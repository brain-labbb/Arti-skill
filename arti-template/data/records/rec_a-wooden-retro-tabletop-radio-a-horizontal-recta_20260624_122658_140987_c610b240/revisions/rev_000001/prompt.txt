A wooden retro tabletop radio. A horizontal rectangular cherry-wood veneer cabinet with softly rounded edges and a glossy finish. The front face has a recessed cream cloth-and-ribbed speaker grille panel (framed in black) with a circular speaker behind it. Below the grille sits a horizontal tuning-dial scale with red and black AM/FM frequency markings, flanked by two round chrome knobs (left tuning, right volume).
