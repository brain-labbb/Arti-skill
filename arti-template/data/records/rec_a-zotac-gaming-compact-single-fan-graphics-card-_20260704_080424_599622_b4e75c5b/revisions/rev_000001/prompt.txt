A ZOTAC Gaming compact single-fan graphics card, ITX-length (about 0.17 m long, 0.11 m tall including the PCIe bracket, roughly 40 mm thick dual-slot). A dark gunmetal plastic shroud dominates the face, with one large central axial cooling fan (~90 mm) whose curved black blades spin around a glossy circular hub badge carrying a white ZOTAC logo. The left and right thirds of the shroud face are covered in genuinely modeled diagonal vent slats, and thin copper/amber accent strips separate the slatted flanks from the central fan zone; a 'ZOTAC GAMING' lettering strip sits in the top-left corner. The fan sits recessed in a circular fan well, with a silver finned aluminum heatsink visible behind the blades. Below the shroud is a dark green-black PCB with a gold PCIe edge connector along the bottom, and a stamped metal PCIe mounting bracket with display-port openings caps one end. Primary articulation: the fan rotor spins continuously about its axis.
