Model a brushed stainless single-lever basin faucet, about 0.20 m tall. The body is one vertical cylinder (about 0.055 m diameter) with a flat top, standing on a slightly wider round base flange. From the front of the body at mid-height, a flat-bottomed channel spout projects forward and slightly down about 0.13 m, with an open trough-like curved tip. On the right side near the top, a round flat disc (about 0.055 m diameter, marked with small red and blue paint dots at top and bottom) is mounted on a short horizontal boss; a slim cylindrical lever bar runs forward from the disc, parallel under the body top, about 0.14 m long. All surfaces are satin brushed steel. Articulation: the disc-and-lever assembly is a revolute joint rotating about the horizontal left-right axis of the disc boss, range -40 to +40 degrees, sweeping the lever tip up and down to control flow; the same assembly has a second revolute about the lever's own forward axis, -30 to +30 degrees, for temperature mix.
