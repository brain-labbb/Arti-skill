Model a polished-chrome self-closing (timed-flow) basin pillar tap, about 0.13 m tall. A stout cylindrical body (about 0.05 m diameter) leans back a few degrees from vertical, standing on a round base flange (about 0.06 m diameter) at the deck. Around the body's upper third runs a thin separation groove ring; above it the body steps in slightly, then flares to a large flat round push cap (about 0.06 m diameter, 0.015 m thick) with a brushed flat top — this cap is the press-to-run button. From the body's front at mid-height a short cylindrical spout (about 0.03 m diameter) projects forward and curves smoothly downward about 0.08 m, ending in a flared open outlet rim. All surfaces mirror chrome. Articulation: the push cap is a prismatic joint sliding 0 to 0.008 m along the body's tilted long axis (press down to start timed flow, spring returns); the same cap also has a revolute joint about that axis, -60 to +60 degrees, for temperature adjustment.
