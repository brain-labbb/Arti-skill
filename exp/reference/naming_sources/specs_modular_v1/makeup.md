# Makeup Modular Spec

## Category Binding

- category: `Makeup`
- template slug: `makeup`
- mechanism profile: `portable_palette_carrier`
- export namespace: `Makeup`
- implementation: `agent/templates/makeup.py`
- canonical source map: `articraft_data/picture_expansion/template_source_maps/0611__Makeup.md`

Identity is a portable makeup carrier with a rear-hinged mirrored lid, visible
powder-pan organizers and at least two complete articulated palette carriers.
It must not become an empty case, jewelry box or ordinary drawer cabinet.

## Source-backed candidates

The exact accepted record/revision/line spans are authoritative in the source
map. Production executes nine complete accepted bundles from these families:

- origins: black fold-out case and pink multi-tier case;
- complete case-form anchors: `train_case`, `book_folio`, `round_compact`,
  `cylindrical_vanity`;
- complete count anchors: N=3, N=5 and N=7 drawer bundles, in addition to the
  N=2 origin/form families;
- coupled parameters: source scale, host-axis scales and source-backed
  prismatic travel; all apply to the complete source bundle (shell, cavity,
  lid, organizers, hardware, collision proxies and joint origins);
- standalone topology/interface forks remain source evidence but are not
  ordinary candidates until their own collision proxies pass full-range sweep.

The standalone `flip_over_leaf` record remains an upstream anchor but is not an
ordinary production candidate until its independent swept-volume blocker is
physically repaired. `cylindrical_vanity` is admitted only as its complete
source-bound PRISMATIC bundle and is never cross-paired with another topology.

## Form Dependency Contracts

| family | master descriptor | coupled consumers | validator |
|---|---|---|---|
| train case | resolved `hx,hy,hz` | rounded host, openings, lid/mirror, hinge, drawer guides or palette posts, closure and pan grid | closed/mid/max motion plus host containment |
| book folio | shallow `hx,hy,hz` | folio shell, lid/mirror, deployment mouth, fan/accordion support and interfaces | shallow-profile ratio and carrier clearance |
| round compact | radius and height | circular body/lid/mirror, radial opening/support and round wells | X/Y congruence and radial swept clearance |

Changing `source_scale` must update every consumer above. Mirror and lid remain
the source-family profiles; a source bundle is never mixed with a topology from
another record. N=2/3/5/7 are resolved from complete accepted source records;
N remains a separately audited multiplicity axis.

## Mechanism Structure

- Lid: complete molded lid, inset mirror, catch and captured rear hinge hardware;
  REVOLUTE signed X from closed 0 to the resolved open angle.
- Drawer: bottom, front, rear, left/right walls and two longitudinal runners;
  parent has matching bilateral guides and a real support deck; PRISMATIC
  outward `[0, resolved_travel]`.
- Fan carrier: complete rimmed pan tray and captured boss on a supported
  vertical post; REVOLUTE Z with a height-derived stack.
- Accordion carrier: complete rimmed trays in a captured indexed chain;
  each child boss mates to the previous real tip post.

No facade-only drawer, transverse fake bar, central fake rail, floating palette
or whole-part overlap allowance is legal.

## InterfaceSpec / MatingContract

Host and carrier interfaces are coupled inside one resolved configuration.
Exact intentional contacts are restricted to lid barrel/pin/knuckles,
drawer runner/matching guide, fan boss/post, and accordion boss/matching tip
post. Every allowance names both elements and leaves all other collisions live.

## Compatibility Gates

- Pull-out drawers: train case only in ordinary production.
- Book folio: fan or accordion; snap closure; N≤5, with accordion N≤3.
- Round compact: fan or accordion; snap closure, brush channels, round wells;
  N∈{2,3}.
- Train case: pull-out, fan or accordion; both closure/storage/pan interface
  candidates; pull-out/fan admit N∈{2,3,5,7}, accordion admits N∈{2,3,5}.
- Cylindrical vanity is source-bound to its PRISMATIC drawer bundle; flip-over
  remains excluded as stated above.

## Combination Domain

- diversity profile: `constrained`.
- core axis: exact complete source record.
- multiplicity axis: `palette_count={2,3,5,7}`; N never inflates core count.
- continuous axes: coupled source scale and source-backed travel scale;
  continuous values never inflate core count.
- ordinary structural domain is nine complete source records. Its honest core
  count is 9; the constrained floor is handled only by a user-approved,
  domain-hash-bound exception. Rejected topology/interface forks are not counted.
- ordinary seeds remain parameterized by source bundle plus continuous values;
  the non-geometric `name` field is excluded from uniqueness checks.

## Swept-clearance and test plan

- Run current-pose collision and disconnected-island checks.
- Sample all non-FIXED joints; no broad allowance.
- Assert lid top rises at the open witness.
- Assert every drawer translates by a meaningful fraction of resolved travel.
- Assert every revolute palette center moves visibly.
- Assert every closed carrier stays inside the resolved host envelope.
- Cover each form, mechanism, N boundary and compatibility gate in corner seeds.

Current evidence: source-faithful seeds 0–5 compile/sweep pass 6/6 after the
source collision-direction repair. VTK smoke renders are non-black; schema v3
final evidence, human approval and formal export remain pending.

## Visual Risk

Risks: `drawer`, `multi_joint`, `dense_multiplicity`, `form_dependency`, and
`source_identity`. Smoke review must deliberately include one source-like
train case and one compact/folio mechanism view. Per seed use closed-front and
max-front only unless one local mechanism view is necessary.

## Reject cases

- seed changes only an unexecuted topology label, name, finish or color;
- mirror/frame/host outlines do not co-vary;
- N does not change emitted complete moving carriers;
- drawer lacks five walls or bilateral longitudinal guidance;
- sibling leaves collide at closed, mid or max pose;
- circular host receives rectangular-only interfaces;
- any fixed-record rotation bypasses the resolved parameter geometry;
- any whole-part overlap allowance or test-shaped support.
