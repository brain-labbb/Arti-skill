category_scope: One hand-powered line winch built as a single grounded support frame carrying exactly one rotating line drum, a ratchet ring the drum turns against, at least one frame-mounted pawl that engages that ring, and one hand crank that reaches outboard of the frame to a hand grip. The frame always presents a bolted mounting interface (base flange, mounting foot or deck flange) and always carries the drum on real bearing stations, so the drum axis stands clear of that mounting plane by more than the largest rotating radius. Both drum-axis orientations in the pool are in scope: horizontal between/beside cheek plates, and vertical on a deck flange (capstan). Powered/electric windlasses, plain rope pulleys and sheaves with no pawl, cleats, and turnbuckles are out of scope: they either drop the ratchet-and-pawl load path or drop the manual crank.
