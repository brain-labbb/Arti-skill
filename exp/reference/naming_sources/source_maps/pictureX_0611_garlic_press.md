category_scope: Handheld two-arm hinged garlic press. The host is the grounded lower arm: a long hand grip that carries a perforated pressing chamber and the hinge lugs at the nose. An upper lever arm is hinged to it on a lateral cross-pin and carries the plunger that enters that chamber. One cleaning / ejection / retention accessory rides on the assembly. Out of scope: rotary drum graters and twist mincers (no hinged lever), rocking mincers, bench-mounted presses, and every loose prop photographed beside the tool.
