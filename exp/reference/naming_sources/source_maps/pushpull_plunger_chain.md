category_scope: Grounded push-pull plunger mechanism: a fixed support/guide host that captures one or more coaxial sliding plungers, plus an optional hinged output member driven at the end of the stroke. Every sliding member stays engaged in a real bore or rail over the full stroke. Rotary-driven screws, rack-and-pinion drives, and free-floating pistons without a guide are outside the category.
