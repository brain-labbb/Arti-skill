# point_of_sale_terminal — SourceMap

source_map_schema: 1
export_category: point_of_sale_terminal
picture_category: Retail_Shop Fixtures
picture_subcategory: Point-of-sale terminal

category_scope: One self-contained electronic payment terminal that reads a card and takes an entry. A single wedge housing is the primary body; it always carries a display and a payment-entry face on its sloped top, a card interface cut into the front face and right side, and a rear receipt-printer compartment closed by a hinged cover. The housing is either grounded on its own rubber feet or seated on exactly one support root (a countertop cradle it reclines in, or a pedestal it yaws on). Cash registers with a drawer, self-checkout kiosk towers, tablets/phones, handheld barcode scanners and bare mPOS card-reader dongles change the host topology or drop the display/entry face and are out of scope.
