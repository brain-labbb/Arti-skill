category_scope: Grounded two-revolute serial arm: one root/mount body, a shoulder joint, an upper link, an elbow joint, a forearm, and a terminal output face. Both revolute axes are parallel to each other and carried by a real bearing interface (clevis cheeks, bearing rings, bosses, or a bearing disc). Wrist roll, additional joints beyond the elbow, gantry rails, and parallelogram linkage arms are outside the category.
