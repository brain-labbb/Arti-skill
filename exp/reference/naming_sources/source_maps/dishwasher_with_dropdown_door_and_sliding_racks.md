## Semantic decisions

- `front_family` intentionally couples cabinet silhouette, door panel, hinge convention and control
  placement.  Treating hinge direction as an independent slot caused the old template's invalid
  coordinate mixing and is rejected.
- Every family exposes the same bottom-front X hinge, hollow tub opening, indexed side-rail planes
  and central Z spray axes.  Rack and wash candidates therefore form a complete Cartesian product
  without compatibility gates.
- `rack_count` is the only multiplicity axis.  Two means lower+upper baskets; three adds a shallow
  top cutlery tray and a third derived rail pair.  Repeated wires, tines, buttons and jets are local
  detail and do not inflate core/raw diversity.
- Door skin, liner pan, gasket, detergent/rinse details and handles remain layered geometry rather
  than a single slab.  Every rack remains an open basket with two side runners; no solid tray or
  unsupported floating grid is accepted.
- The host derives rack width, depth, level spacing, rail spacing, slide travel, hinge placement and
  spray-arm span from common tub dimensions.  Parameter changes must preserve containment and
  motion clearance.
