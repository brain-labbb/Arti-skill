    \footnotesize
    \textit{Protocol summary.}
    In (a1), each asset is reset three times and simulated for 10\,s at 240\,Hz
    with a fixed root, gravity, contacts, self-collision, and zero applied
    joint force. In (a2), the root is free, movable joints are locked at
    canonical $q=0$, and the asset is simulated for 10\,s under gravity; its
    denominator is the eligible free-standing cohort. The asterisked (a2)
    metrics are conditional on $N_{\mathrm{phys}}$. In (b), All-3 denotes
    the exact intersection of assets or joints passing in Genesis, PyBullet,
    and MuJoCo, rather than an average of simulator-level rates. Full protocol
    and failure-handling details are provided in the appendix.
