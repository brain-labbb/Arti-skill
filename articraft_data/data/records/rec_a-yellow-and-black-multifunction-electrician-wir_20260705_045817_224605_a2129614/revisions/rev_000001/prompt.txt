A yellow-and-black multifunction electrician wire-stripping pliers. Long pointed serrated nose, blades with graduated wire-stripping notches (1.0, 1.5, 2.5, 4.0 mm), a crimping section near the pivot, brushed steel blade faces on dark textured steel arms, yellow soft-grip handles with black overmold, a coil compression spring between the handles, and a small thumb lock latch. Squeezing the handles closes the jaws about the central pivot.
