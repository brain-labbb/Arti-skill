Model a decorative Japanese katana display set: a black lacquered two-tier wooden sword stand mounted on a rectangular black base box (about 0.30 m wide, 0.18 m deep, 0.12 m tall) bearing a gold-framed white kanji plaque. The stand has two upright pillars with curved cradle notches holding two horizontal sheathed katanas, and a third katana rests across the base box top. Each katana is about 1.0 m long: a gently curved cylindrical scabbard (saya) in glossy white with pink cherry-blossom and pink-band decoration, a pink diamond-wrapped handle (tsuka) about 0.25 m long with a dark pommel, an ornate dark-red disc guard (tsuba), and a polished silver steel blade hidden inside the scabbard. Articulation: each of the three blades (handle, tsuba, and blade as one rigid unit) slides out of its scabbard along the scabbard's long axis via a prismatic joint, travel 0 to about 0.7 m, independently for each sword.
