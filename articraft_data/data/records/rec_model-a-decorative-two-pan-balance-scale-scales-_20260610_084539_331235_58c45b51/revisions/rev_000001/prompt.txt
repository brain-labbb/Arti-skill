Model a decorative two-pan balance scale (scales-of-justice style), about 0.40 m tall, 0.35 m wide across the beam, made entirely of matte black cast iron. Parts: a square stepped plinth base (~0.13 m wide) with a fluted ring detail; a turned baluster column rising from the base with several ring moldings, thicker near the bottom and tapering upward, topped by a small dome finial; a straight horizontal cross beam (~0.30 m long, rectangular section) mounted just below the finial; and two shallow round dished pans (~0.11 m diameter, slightly concave with a thin rim) suspended from the beam ends by three simplified chain hangers each (model the chains as thin rigid angled rods or stylized link strands converging from the pan rim to a hook at the beam tip). Articulation: the beam pivots on the column top via a revolute joint about a horizontal axis perpendicular to the beam, range about -15 to +15 degrees (the weighing tilt). Each pan-and-hanger assembly attaches to its beam tip with its own revolute joint about the same horizontal axis direction, range -15 to +15 degrees, letting the pans swing and stay level as the beam tilts.
