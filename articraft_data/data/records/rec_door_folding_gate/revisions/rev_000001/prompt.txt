A concertina / accordion trellis security gate (an expanding scissor gate) mounted inside a rectangular steel frame, painted terracotta-salmon. The gate is a pantograph lattice of flat steel bars crossed and riveted at their intersections into a repeating diamond pattern, with thin vertical pickets. It folds and collapses to one side like an accordion so the diamonds compress. Model the scissor mechanism with revolute pivots at the bar crossings so the lattice genuinely expands and contracts; the surrounding frame is fixed. Reference image: picture/Door/Folding gate/001.png
