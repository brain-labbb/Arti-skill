Model a free-standing top-freezer refrigerator, approximately 1.75 m tall, 0.65 m wide and 0.68 m deep, finished in glossy black with a slightly rounded front face. The body is a hollow insulated cabinet split into two compartments: a shorter freezer compartment occupying the top ~30% and a taller fresh-food compartment below, separated by a thin horizontal mullion gap. A short recessed dark-gray plinth/kick panel sits at the bottom front. Each compartment has its own full-width hinged door with a horizontal brushed-aluminum bar handle mounted near the door's lower edge (freezer door) and upper edge (fridge door), close to the compartment split. A small silver brand badge sits on the upper freezer door. Articulation: the freezer door is a revolute joint about a vertical axis at the right front edge of the cabinet, range 0 to ~120 degrees opening outward; the fridge door is an independent revolute joint about the same vertical right-edge axis line, also 0 to ~120 degrees.
