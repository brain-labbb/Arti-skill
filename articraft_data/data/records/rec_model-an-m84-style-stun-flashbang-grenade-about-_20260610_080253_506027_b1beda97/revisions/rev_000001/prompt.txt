Model an M84-style stun (flashbang) grenade, about 0.13 m tall and 0.05 m in diameter. The body is a vertical olive-green steel cylinder perforated by three rows of large circular vent holes (about 0.012 m diameter, 4-5 holes per row around the circumference) revealing a tan/beige inner charge tube; one lower hole shows a reddish insert, and a darker brown band wraps the middle row. Top and bottom ends are chunky hexagonal olive-green caps, the upper one stenciled '01-0009' in white. On top sits a weathered silver fuze block with a folded dark-green safety lever running down one side, secured by TWO separate stainless wire pull pins, each with its own triangular/round bent-wire pull ring (about 0.035 m across) splayed at angles. Articulations: (1) safety lever, revolute at the fuze-top pivot, horizontal axis, swinging ~90 degrees outward; (2) primary pull pin with ring, prismatic, sliding ~0.02 m along its horizontal axis out of the fuze block; (3) secondary safety pin with ring, prismatic on a different horizontal axis, same ~0.02 m travel; each ring is a revolute child of its pin, swinging ~120 degrees.
