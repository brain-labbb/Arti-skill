Model a vintage hurricane (kerosene) lantern, about 0.28 m tall and 0.14 m in base diameter. The body is glossy dark-green painted metal: a round stepped fount base at the bottom, a barrel-shaped clear glass globe in the middle (a small orange flame visible inside on a wick), a green collar ring above and below the globe, two curved vertical green side tubes connecting base to top, and a domed green chimney cap with a perforated vent band under a flared top disk. A thin brass/gold wire bail handle arcs over the lantern, its two ends hooked into pivot ears on the side tubes near the top; in the image it hangs swung down to one side. Articulations: (1) bail handle, revolute joint about the horizontal axis through the two pivot ears, range about -100 to +100 degrees so it can swing from resting against one side, up over the top, and down the other side; (2) wick-adjustment knob, a small knurled disk protruding from the fount base, revolute (continuous) about its horizontal protruding axis to raise/lower the wick. Keep the glass globe slightly transparent and the metal parts hollow shells. Reference image: picture/Light/Latern/001.png
