Model a bicycle-style quick-release seat clamp in brushed silver aluminum, monochrome metallic throughout. Overall size roughly 0.09 m long, 0.05 m wide, 0.03 m tall. The body is a split ring collar (hollow cylindrical band, inner bore about 0.032 m diameter, band height about 0.015 m, wall about 0.005 m thick) with a slit gap and two flat boss lugs flanking the gap. A cross bolt passes through the lugs. On one side sits a sculpted quick-release handle: a solid one-piece mesh with a gently curved blade about 0.06 m long and a swept pointed tip, attached at the side pivot while the cross bolt and cam barrel remain fixed hardware. On the opposite side the bolt ends in a knurled cylindrical adjuster barrel nut, about 0.022 m diameter and 0.02 m long, with fine vertical knurling ridges. Articulations: (1) the solid quick-release handle rotates about the vertical Z axis at its side pivot as a revolute joint with about 0 to 170 degrees travel between closed and fully open, rather than rotating the middle cross bolt; (2) the knurled adjuster nut spins about the bolt axis as a continuous joint for tension adjustment.
