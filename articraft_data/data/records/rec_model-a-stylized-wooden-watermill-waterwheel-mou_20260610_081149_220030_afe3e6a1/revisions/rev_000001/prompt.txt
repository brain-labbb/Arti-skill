Model a stylized wooden watermill waterwheel mounted on a freestanding A-frame trestle, about 2.2 m tall overall with a wheel roughly 1.8 m in diameter and 0.5 m wide. The wheel has two parallel circular rims in pale beige-pink wood, built from thick segmented arcs, joined by eight to ten flat rectangular paddle boards spanning between the rims around the circumference like ladder rungs. Each rim has straight radial spokes (about six per side) converging on a central hub. A light-gray metal axle passes through both hubs, capped on each outer end with a short splined/ribbed cylindrical collar resembling a gear sleeve. The wheel is carried between two A-frame supports in darker grayish-brown wood: each side has two legs angled outward to square block feet, plus a horizontal cross brace partway down and a diagonal brace connecting the frames front to back. Articulation: the entire wheel assembly (rims, spokes, paddles, hub) rotates as one rigid body on a continuous revolute joint about the horizontal axle axis, spinning freely 360 degrees relative to the static trestle frame.
