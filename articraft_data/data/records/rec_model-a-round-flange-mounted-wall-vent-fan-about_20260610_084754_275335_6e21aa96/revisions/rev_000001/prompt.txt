Model a round flange-mounted wall vent fan, about 0.26 m outer diameter and 0.08 m deep, in pale grey painted metal. The front is a wide flat annular mounting flange with 8 small hex bolt heads evenly spaced around its rim, surrounding a raised circular collar. Inside the collar opening sits a radial array of about 8 flat trapezoidal shutter/impeller blades arranged like a pinwheel around a small central hub disc, with narrow gaps between blades; behind them is a shallow hollow drum housing. Parts: bolted flange ring and collar (fixed), the bladed impeller disc, and one representative backdraft shutter flap behind it. Articulations: (1) continuous joint — the impeller disc with its 8 pinwheel blades rotates about the horizontal center axis normal to the flange, unlimited rotation; (2) revolute joint — a thin circular backdraft flap inside the rear of the drum pivots on a horizontal hinge across its top edge, swinging open rearward 0 to about 80 degrees. Keep the drum hollow so the impeller is visible from the front. Reference image: picture/Other/Vent/004.png
