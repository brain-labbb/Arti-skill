A classic communications satellite in orbit: a rectangular bus wrapped in gold foil-like multilayer insulation panels, with two large flat solar array wings extending symmetrically from opposite sides, each wing made of several blue rectangular photovoltaic panels in a row with a visible cell grid, attached to the bus through a silver boom and yoke driven by a solar array drive so each wing can rotate about its boom axis. The Earth-facing face carries a small parabolic dish antenna on a gimbal plus a feed horn cluster and a few slim rod antennas.
