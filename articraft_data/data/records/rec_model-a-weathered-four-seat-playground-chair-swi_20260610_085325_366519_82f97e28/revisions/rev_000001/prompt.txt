Model a weathered four-seat playground chair-swing carousel, about 3.0 m in diameter and 1.3 m tall. A short rusty red-and-white painted steel center column (about 0.12 m diameter, 1.2 m tall) sits on a small square base plate and carries a rotating hub topped by a flat white disc cap with a red center. From the hub, four straight tubular arms (about 1.4 m long, painted alternating bright blue and yellow, chipped and rusty) radiate horizontally, cross-braced by diagonal tubes that form an X-truss between neighboring arms. At each arm tip hangs a flat rectangular sheet-metal seat platform (about 0.45 x 0.35 m, faded red/brown, rusted) with a low tubular backrest hoop, suspended about 0.5 m above ground by a short bracket. Articulation: the entire hub-arm-seat rotor spins on the center column via a continuous joint about the vertical Z axis; each of the four seats additionally pivots on a revolute joint at its arm-tip bracket about a horizontal axis tangential to the spin circle, swinging roughly -30 to +30 degrees so seats fly outward as the carousel turns.
