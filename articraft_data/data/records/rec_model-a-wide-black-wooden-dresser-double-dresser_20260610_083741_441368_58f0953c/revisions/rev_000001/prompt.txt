Model a wide black wooden dresser (double dresser), approximately 1.7 m wide, 0.85 m tall, 0.5 m deep. The carcass and drawer fronts are matte black wood; the flat overhanging top panel reads as smooth silver-gray. The front corners feature decorative vertical posts carved with a stacked spiral/faceted zigzag pattern, continuing down into four straight square legs about 0.15 m tall. The front holds eight drawers in three rows: a top row of four small drawers (each one knob), then two rows of two wide drawers (each wide drawer has two knobs). Knobs are small round polished silver/white balls on short stems. Each of the eight drawers is an independent prismatic joint sliding horizontally outward along the depth axis (perpendicular to the front face), range 0 to about 0.4 m; model each drawer as a hollow open-top box with its front panel slightly proud of the carcass. Keep drawer fronts flat with thin reveal gaps between them, and give the silver top a small overhang on all sides. Reference image: picture/Other/Cabinet/002.png
