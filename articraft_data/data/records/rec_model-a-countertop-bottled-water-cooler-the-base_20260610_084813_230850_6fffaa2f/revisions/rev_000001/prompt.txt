Model a countertop bottled water cooler. The base unit is a boxy white-and-light-gray plastic cabinet about 0.30 m wide, 0.33 m deep and 0.38 m tall, with softly rounded vertical edges, a side ventilation grille, and a recessed dispensing alcove on the front lower half containing a gray drip-tray grille at its floor. Above the alcove sits a glossy black control panel with a red LED temperature readout. On top, a translucent blue 19-liter (5-gallon) water bottle is mounted inverted into a collar; the bottle is about 0.27 m diameter and 0.35 m tall with ribbed rings and a tapering neck into the dispenser. Inside the alcove ceiling are two small dispensing taps with push paddles (one red-marked hot, one blue-marked cold). Articulation: each paddle tap is an independent revolute joint about a horizontal left-right axis at the top of the paddle, swinging rearward 0 to about 30 degrees when a cup pushes it. Optionally make the drip tray a shallow lift-out piece, but the two paddle taps are the primary joints. Total assembly height roughly 0.75 m. Reference image: picture/Other/Water dispenser/001.png
