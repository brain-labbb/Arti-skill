An NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 Founders Edition graphics card, a massive triple-slot flow-through GPU about 0.30 m long and 0.14 m tall including the PCIe bracket, roughly 60 mm thick. Angular two-tone shroud: a gunmetal silver X-frame wrapping darker near-black inner panels, with a large black center plate carrying the RTX 4090 label and GEFORCE RTX lettering along the side rail. Dual axial cooling fans in the signature flow-through layout: one fan set into the TOP face near the tail of the card, the other set into the UNDERSIDE near the bracket end, each spinning continuously on its own axis so air passes straight through the finned tail. Dark angular heatsink fin block visible through the flow-through cutout at the tail (the short FE PCB does not extend behind the tail fan). Black backplate, silver PCIe mounting bracket with three DisplayPort openings and one HDMI opening, and a gold PCIe edge connector along the bottom of the PCB section.
