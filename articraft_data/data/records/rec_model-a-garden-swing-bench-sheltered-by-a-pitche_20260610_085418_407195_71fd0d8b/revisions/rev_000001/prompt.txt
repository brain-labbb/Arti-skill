Model a garden swing bench sheltered by a pitched roof. Overall about 2.0 m wide, 1.9 m tall, 1.4 m deep. The structure is light natural pine: two A-frame end walls, each filled with vertical slats (open lattice, gaps between slats) and a small triangular slatted gable at the top, joined by a ridge beam and roof purlins. The pitched roof is sage-green corrugated metal sheeting overhanging the frame on all sides, with visible ridges running down the slope. Beneath the roof hangs a slatted wooden bench swing (~1.5 m wide) with a flat slat seat and a reclined slat backrest, suspended at each end by thin metal chains or rods from the frame near the roof line. Treat the bench (seat, backrest, armrest ends, and both hangers) as one rigid pendulum body. Articulation: the bench connects to the frame with a single revolute joint, axis horizontal and parallel to the ridge beam, located at the upper hanger anchor points; swing range about -0.6 to +0.6 rad fore-aft.
