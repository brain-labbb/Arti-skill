Model a modern winged swivel lounge armchair with a matching ottoman, shown in grey fabric upholstery with tan/brown leather-look outer shells. Chair footprint about 0.85 m wide x 0.85 m deep x 1.05 m tall; ottoman about 0.6 x 0.45 x 0.4 m. The chair has a deep curved bucket seat shell whose backrest flares into two angled wing panels at the top; cushions are softly rounded boxes. The shell sits on a matte-black metal pedestal: a short vertical column splaying into a 4-spoke flat star base (spokes taper toward the floor tips). The ottoman is a rounded rectangular cushion on its own identical 4-spoke black star base. Articulation: (1) the entire chair shell rotates on the pedestal column via a continuous joint about the vertical Z axis; (2) the backrest/wing shell reclines relative to the seat via a revolute joint about a horizontal left-right axis near the seat-back junction, range 0 to -0.3 rad (lean back). Build the ottoman as a fixed companion body offset in front of the chair. Use boxes and tapered/rotated prisms for spokes; keep cushions slightly inflated-looking via chamfered boxes.
