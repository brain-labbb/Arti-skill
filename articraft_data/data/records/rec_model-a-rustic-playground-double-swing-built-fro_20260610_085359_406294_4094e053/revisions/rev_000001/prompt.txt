Model a rustic playground double swing built from round timber logs. The frame is about 2.6 m wide and 2.1 m tall: two pairs of thick cylindrical log posts (each pair slightly splayed, lashed together near the top) support a single horizontal log crossbeam; four small brass/gold finial caps sit on top of the beam. All wood is a warm natural brown with visible grain. Two identical swings hang from the beam, each suspended by two twisted tan ropes about 1.4 m long with decorative knots, ending at a flat wooden plank seat (~0.5 m wide, 0.25 m deep) with a thin raised back rail and brass corner fittings. Treat each swing (both ropes plus seat) as one rigid pendulum link. Articulation: each swing attaches to the crossbeam with its own revolute joint, axis horizontal and parallel to the crossbeam, located at the rope hanger points; range about -1.0 to +1.0 rad of fore-aft swing, the two swings fully independent.
