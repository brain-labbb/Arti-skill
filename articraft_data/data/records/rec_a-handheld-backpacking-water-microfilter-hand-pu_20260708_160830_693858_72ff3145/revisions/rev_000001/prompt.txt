A handheld backpacking water microfilter hand pump: an olive-green twin-barrel plastic housing (a taller rear pump cylinder fused to a shorter front filter-cartridge cylinder, joined by a shared wall) with a black textured rubber grip pad on the front seam and fine ribbed grip lines on the pump cylinder sides. A straight cylindrical piston rod topped by a flattened oval T-bar handle plugs into a fluted collar on top of the pump cylinder and reciprocates up and down (prismatic slide) to work the internal piston. A ridged, fluted twist-off cap sits on top of the shorter filter cylinder (revolute twist to unlock for cartridge service) and carries the filtered-water outlet barb, from which a clear flexible tube runs out and up to a small blue outlet clip. A raw-water intake barb near the base of the rear cylinder feeds a clear flexible intake hose that loops down and away to a small dark foam pre-filter tip. Overall height about 17 cm body plus an extending handle, roughly 4 cm across each barrel; matte plastic olive-green body, black rubber grip, translucent hoses.
