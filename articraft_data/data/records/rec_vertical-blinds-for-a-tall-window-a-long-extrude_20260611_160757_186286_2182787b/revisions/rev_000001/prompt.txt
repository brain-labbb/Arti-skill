Vertical blinds for a tall window: a long extruded-aluminum headrail with a row of small carrier hooks, gray fabric vertical vanes hanging side by side with slight overlap, and a control wand with chain hanging at the left end. Primary articulation: the vanes tilt in unison about their vertical axes to open/close the light gap (use a mimic chain driven by one vane), and the vane set can traverse along the headrail.
