Model a classic disposable flint pocket lighter (BIC style), about 0.08 m tall, 0.025 m wide, 0.012 m deep, standing upright. The body is a glossy royal-blue plastic fuel reservoir: a rounded-rectangle prism with semicircular ends in plan, smooth and slightly translucent. On top sits a polished chrome sheet-metal hood/wind guard wrapping the upper 0.015 m, with an open notch at the front where the flame exits and small perforations optional. Centered on top is a knurled steel spark wheel about 0.010 m diameter and 0.008 m wide, its ridged rim protruding above the hood; flank it with two thin chrome side cheeks. Behind/below the wheel is the dark-grey plastic fuel lever (gas button), a small curved thumb tab. Articulations: (1) spark wheel, continuous joint about a horizontal axis running left-right through the wheel center, free spinning to strike the flint; (2) fuel lever, revolute joint about a parallel horizontal axis at the lever's rear pivot inside the hood, pressing down about 12 degrees (0 to -0.21 rad) to open the valve. Keep the reservoir hollow-looking with a visible wall at the open top edge. The reference also shows a red color variant lying down; build the blue upright unit.
