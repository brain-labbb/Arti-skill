Model a compact freestanding gas range cooker, approximately 0.50 m wide, 0.60 m deep, and 0.85 m tall. The body is a white enamel-coated steel box on four short feet. The flat top holds four gas burners: each burner is a small silver cap on a recessed base, overlaid by black cast-iron pan grates with thin square bars. Below the cooktop, the white front fascia carries a horizontal row of about six round white control knobs. The lower two-thirds of the front is a full-width oven door with a large dark tinted-glass window framed in white, and a slim horizontal handle bar near its top edge. Articulations: (1) the oven door is a revolute drop-down door hinged along its bottom horizontal edge (axis parallel to the front, left-right), swinging from closed (vertical) to fully open about 90 degrees; (2) each control knob is a revolute dial rotating about a horizontal axis perpendicular to the front panel, range roughly 0 to 270 degrees.
