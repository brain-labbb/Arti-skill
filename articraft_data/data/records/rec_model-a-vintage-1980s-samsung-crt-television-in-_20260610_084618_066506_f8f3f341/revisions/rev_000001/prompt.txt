Model a vintage 1980s Samsung CRT television in a rectangular wood-grain cabinet, approximately 0.62 m wide, 0.45 m tall, 0.32 m deep. The cabinet is a hollow box of light tan/beige weathered wood veneer with visible grain and worn edges. The front is dominated by a large recessed CRT screen on the left two-thirds: a pale greenish-grey curved glass tube with rounded corners, set inside a slightly darker beige plastic bezel inset from the cabinet face. Articulation: the channel-selector dial is a continuous joint spinning about a horizontal axis normal to the front face; the three small knobs are revolute joints about the same axis orientation, each rotating roughly -135 to +135 degrees for volume/brightness/tuning.
