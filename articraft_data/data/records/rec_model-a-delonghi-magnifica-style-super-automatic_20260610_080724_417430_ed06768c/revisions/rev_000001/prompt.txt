Model a DeLonghi Magnifica-style super-automatic bean-to-cup espresso machine, roughly 0.43 m tall, 0.24 m wide, 0.35 m deep. The body is a boxy upright form in two-tone dark gray and graphite-black plastic: a lighter gray angled control fascia wraps the upper front, flanked by glossy black side panels, with a flat black top deck containing a perforated cup-warming grid and a hinged bean-hopper lid. The fascia carries a large central rotary dial with a metallic silver ring plus small square buttons and printed icons. Below the fascia, a black coffee dispensing spout block with two chrome nozzles rides on vertical rails between the gray cheeks. A cylindrical stainless steam wand angles down from the left side. At the base sits a perforated stainless drip-tray plate over a black tray. Articulations: (1) rotary strength/volume dial, continuous joint about a horizontal axis normal to the tilted fascia; (2) dispensing spout block, prismatic joint sliding vertically about 0.06 m for cup height; (3) drip tray, prismatic joint pulling forward about 0.12 m for emptying; (4) steam wand, revolute joint at its upper mount swinging about 60 degrees about a vertical axis; (5) bean-hopper lid on top, revolute hinge at its rear edge opening about 100 degrees.
