Model a pair of short, stout blacksmith pincers (nipper-style tongs) in aged dark gray steel with worn, polished highlights on edges. Overall length about 0.30 m, much chunkier than ordinary tongs. The head consists of two thick, inward-curving jaws that hook toward each other and meet at a narrow biting edge at the top, forming a rounded, claw-like profile about 0.06 m wide and 0.05 m tall with a visible gap between the jaw backs. Just below the head the two halves overlap at a broad flattened boss roughly 0.045 m in diameter with a large domed rivet head at its center. Two robust handles extend downward about 0.22 m, gently bowed outward then tapering to slightly flared, rounded tips; cross-section is a rounded rectangle about 0.018 m thick near the boss thinning toward the ends. Surfaces show hammered forge texture. Articulation: a single revolute joint at the rivet, axis normal to the tool plane; squeezing the handles together closes the curved jaws.
