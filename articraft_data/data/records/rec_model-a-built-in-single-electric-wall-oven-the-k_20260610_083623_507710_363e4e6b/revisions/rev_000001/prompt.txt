Model a built-in single electric wall oven, the kind flush-mounted into kitchen cabinetry. Overall fascia approximately 0.60 m wide x 0.60 m tall x 0.55 m deep (the body is a hollow box recessed behind the front frame). All exterior surfaces are matte light-gray painted steel. The top fifth of the fascia is a fixed control strip: a dark glass touch panel with a centered rectangular display reading a clock time, flanked by small printed touch icons on both sides. Below it, a large drop-down oven door fills the remaining front: a gray door frame surrounding a big near-square inset window of frosted/white glass, with a brushed-aluminum bar handle spanning the door near its top edge, standing off on two short posts. The door is the primary articulation: a revolute joint along its bottom horizontal edge (axis parallel to the oven width), swinging outward and down from 0 (closed, vertical) to about 90 degrees (horizontal, open), revealing a hollow oven cavity inside. Optionally include a wire shelf rack inside the cavity on a prismatic slide (pull-out travel about 0.35 m along the depth axis). Reference image: picture/Other/Built-in oven/001.png
