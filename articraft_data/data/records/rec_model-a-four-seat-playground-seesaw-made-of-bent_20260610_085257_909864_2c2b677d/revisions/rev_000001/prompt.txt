Model a four-seat playground seesaw made of bent steel tubing (~40 mm diameter). A central base of sky-blue painted tube forms two arched inverted-U legs joined by short cross members, about 0.7 m tall and showing chipped paint and rust. Across the top of this base sit two independent rocking beams, each a long yellow tube assembly about 2.6 m long, arranged in a shallow X so the two beams cross above the base at slightly different angles. Each yellow beam is a triangulated truss of tube (main top tube plus a diagonal brace tube) and carries at each end: a flat rust-brown rectangular sheet-metal seat plate on a short bent support, and a yellow T-shaped handlebar welded upright just inboard of the seat. Paint is worn with rust along edges. Articulation: each yellow beam connects to the blue base with its own revolute joint, horizontal axis perpendicular to that beam's length at its midpoint, so the two seesaws rock independently about plus/minus 18 degrees. Base is static. Build everything from swept cylinders for tubes and thin boxes for seats. Reference image: picture/Playground/Seesaw/002.png
