Model a single-faucet countertop beverage dispensing tower (draft-style water/beer tap). A rectangular brushed stainless-steel drip tray, about 0.40 m by 0.30 m and 0.03 m tall with rounded corners, has a perforated top plate covered in a grid of small round holes. Near its rear edge a matte dark-gray/black cylindrical column rises vertically, about 0.075 m diameter and 0.35 m tall, with a flared circular base skirt where it meets the tray and a flat polished cap on top. Just below the cap, a chrome faucet body projects forward about 0.08 m, ending in a downward-curved tapered spout. On top of the faucet body sits a glossy black tapered tap handle about 0.12 m long on a chrome lever collar, angled slightly. Articulation: the tap handle plus its faucet lever collar form one revolute joint about a horizontal left-right axis through the faucet body where the handle mounts; pulling the handle forward rotates it from upright (0 degrees) to about 35 degrees forward to dispense, spring-returning closed.
