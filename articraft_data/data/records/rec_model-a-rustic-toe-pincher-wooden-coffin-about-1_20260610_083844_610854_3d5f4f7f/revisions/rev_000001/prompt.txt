Model a rustic toe-pincher wooden coffin, about 1.9 m long, 0.6 m wide across the shoulders tapering to roughly 0.35 m at the head and 0.3 m at the foot, and 0.4 m tall overall. It is built from weathered medium-brown wooden planks with visible grain and uneven plank seams; three darker horizontal wooden strap boards wrap across the lid and body (near the head, across the shoulder break, and near the foot), and a dark-brown wooden Latin cross is mounted on the upper lid panel. The body is a hollow hexagonal (six-sided, toe-pincher) box with walls about 0.03 m thick and an open top; side walls angle outward at the shoulder line. The lid is a matching flat hexagonal plank panel, slightly overhanging the box rim, carrying the cross and the strap boards. Articulation: the lid attaches to the box along one long side edge with a revolute hinge whose axis runs along the coffin's length at the top rim of that side wall; range 0 to about 110 degrees so the lid swings open to reveal the hollow interior.
