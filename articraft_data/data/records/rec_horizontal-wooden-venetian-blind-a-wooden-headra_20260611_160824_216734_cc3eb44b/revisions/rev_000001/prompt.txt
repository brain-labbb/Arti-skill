Horizontal wooden venetian blind: a wooden headrail box, dark wood slats held by fabric ladder tapes, a heavier bottom rail, lift cords running through the slats, and a tilt wand plus pull cord with tassels hanging in front. Primary articulation: slats tilt in unison about their long horizontal axes (mimic chain), and pulling the lift cord raises the slats themselves — they rise and gather from the bottom upward into a tight stack beneath the headrail (the bottom rail is the lift driver; each slat rides its own mimic lift carrier whose travel grows toward the bottom of the blind, so the lowest slats lead the gather), like the part-raised blinds in the photo.
