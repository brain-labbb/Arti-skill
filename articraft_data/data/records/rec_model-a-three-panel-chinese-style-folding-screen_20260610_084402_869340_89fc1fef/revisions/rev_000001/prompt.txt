Model a three-panel Chinese-style folding screen (room divider). Overall: three rectangular panels, each about 0.6 m wide, 1.7 m tall, 0.03 m thick, standing on the floor; the assembly spans roughly 1.6 m when partially folded. Each panel has a dark red-brown lacquered wooden frame (rails about 0.05 m wide, with a slightly wider solid bottom rail around 0.25 m tall) surrounding a matte black inset field decorated with an ornate gold/brass geometric lattice of interlocking rectangles and squares (model the fretwork as thin raised gold bars on the black backing, or a shallow gold grid relief). The center panel is the fixed base link, standing vertically. The left and right panels each attach to the center panel along the shared vertical edge with a revolute hinge: axis vertical (Z), located at the panel seam, range about -150 to +150 degrees so each wing can fold flat against the center panel either way. Pose the wings angled forward roughly 30-40 degrees from coplanar, as in the photo. Keep panels thin slabs with framed borders so the hinge seams read clearly.
