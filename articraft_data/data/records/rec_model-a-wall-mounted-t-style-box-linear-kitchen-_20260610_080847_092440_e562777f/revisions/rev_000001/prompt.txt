Model a wall-mounted T-style (box/linear) kitchen chimney range hood in brushed stainless steel. Overall about 0.9 m wide canopy, 0.5 m deep, and 1.0-1.2 m total height. The bottom canopy is a wide shallow box (~0.9 x 0.5 x 0.12 m) with a flat front fascia carrying a row of five small round push buttons and a tiny red logo; the underside has two recessed round LED lamps near the outer ends and a dark rectangular aluminum mesh grease-filter panel in the center, framed by an angled tapered skirt. From the canopy top rises a slender rectangular chimney duct cover (~0.32 m wide, 0.28 m deep): a fixed lower section topped by a slightly wider telescoping upper sleeve. Articulations: (1) the upper chimney sleeve slides vertically over the lower section on a prismatic joint, axis +Z, range 0 to 0.35 m extension; (2) the internal blower fan disc, visible behind the grease filter, spins on a continuous joint about a vertical axis; (3) one of the front push buttons (the power button) presses inward on a short prismatic joint, axis horizontal into the fascia, range 0 to 0.004 m.
