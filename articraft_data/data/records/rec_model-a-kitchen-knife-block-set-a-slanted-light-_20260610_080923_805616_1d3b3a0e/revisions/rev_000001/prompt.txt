Model a kitchen knife block set: a slanted light-oak wooden block (about 0.13 m wide, 0.12 m deep, 0.24 m tall, leaning back roughly 10-15 degrees) standing on four small dark rubber feet, with an engraved logo panel on the front face. The block holds six knives of varying sizes in angled top slots: each knife has a polished tapered stainless-steel blade, a steel bolster, and a curved dark walnut handle with visible rivets. A pair of kitchen shears with two dark wooden handles and riveted steel blades sits in a wide front slot below the knives. Articulation: each of the six knives is an independent prismatic joint sliding along its own slot axis (tilted with the block, roughly 15 degrees off vertical), travel about 0.12-0.18 m from fully seated to drawn out so the blade clears the slot. The shears are a prismatic joint sliding out of the front slot (travel about 0.10 m) and additionally have a revolute pivot at the central rivet between the two halves, opening the handles about 0-40 degrees.
