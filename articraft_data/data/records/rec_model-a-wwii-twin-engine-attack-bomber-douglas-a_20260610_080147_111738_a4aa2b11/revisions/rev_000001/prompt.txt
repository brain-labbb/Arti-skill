Model a WWII twin-engine attack bomber (Douglas A-26 Invader style) in bare-metal silver/light grey with a yellow band on the tall vertical tail, US star-and-bar insignia on the wing and fuselage, "RG-A" fuselage codes, tail number 322369, red "Stinky" nose art, and olive-drab anti-glare panels on top of the engine nacelles. Length about 15.5 m, wingspan about 21.5 m. Parts: a long round fuselage with a glazed multi-pane cockpit canopy, a gun nose with several small black gun barrels, a domed turret bump aft of the cockpit, and a tapered tail with one tall fin plus horizontal stabilizers; mid-mounted straight wings each carrying a large cylindrical engine nacelle with a chrome cowl ring; each nacelle has a spinner hub with three black propeller blades. Articulation: (1) left propeller, continuous joint spinning about its nacelle's longitudinal axis; (2) right propeller, an independent continuous joint, same axis orientation; (3) the dorsal gun turret rotates on a continuous (or full-revolution revolute) joint about a vertical axis on the fuselage spine.
