Foosball table: black cabinet with rounded-corner side panels, four white splayed legs, green playing field with white pitch markings, and eight telescoping steel player rods crossing the cabinet, each with a colored handle grip and rows of red or blue molded player figures (alternating team rows, goalkeeper rods at the ends). Bead score counters sit above each end. Primary articulation: each rod both slides through the cabinet walls (prismatic) and spins about its axis (revolute on the rod) to kick.
