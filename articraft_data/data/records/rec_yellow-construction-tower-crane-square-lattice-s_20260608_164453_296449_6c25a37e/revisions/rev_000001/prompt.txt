Yellow construction tower crane: square lattice steel mast on a concrete base at ground level, a slewing unit with operator cab on top, a horizontal lattice jib with a traveling trolley plus a counter-jib carrying concrete counterweights, and a suspended hook block. Primary articulation: the upperworks (jib + counter-jib) slews about the vertical mast axis (revolute); the trolley travels horizontally along the jib (prismatic); the hook block raises and lowers (prismatic vertical).
