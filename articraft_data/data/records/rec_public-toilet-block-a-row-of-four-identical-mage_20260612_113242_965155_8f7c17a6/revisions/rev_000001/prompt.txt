Public toilet block: a row of four identical magenta-red molded-plastic portable toilet cabins (porta-potties) standing side by side on a shared base, as in a festival or construction-site sanitation bank. Each cabin has vertical wall ribs, a sloped translucent white roof, a corner vent stack, and a full-height front door with a top vent slot, an occupancy latch indicator, and a handle; each door swings open independently about its own vertical hinge.
