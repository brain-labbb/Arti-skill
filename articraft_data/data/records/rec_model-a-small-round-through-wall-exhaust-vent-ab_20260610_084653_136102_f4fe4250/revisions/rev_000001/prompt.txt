Model a small round through-wall exhaust vent, about 0.16 m outer diameter and 0.07 m deep, in off-white/cream plastic. The body is a short hollow cylindrical duct sleeve with a wider rounded ring bezel (donut-like flange) at the front face that would sit against the wall. Recessed inside the bezel opening is a flat square-mesh wire grille (thin crossing bars forming roughly 8x8 square cells) covering the duct mouth. Behind the grille, inside the duct, sits a small axial fan: a central cylindrical hub with 3-4 broad curved white blades, mounted on a horizontal shaft along the duct axis supported by a thin motor mount strut. Parts: duct sleeve, front bezel ring, mesh grille (all fixed), and the fan impeller. Articulation: one continuous joint — the fan hub plus blades spin about the duct's central horizontal axis (perpendicular to the wall face), unlimited rotation. Keep the duct interior hollow so the spinning blades are visible through the grille. Reference image: picture/Other/Vent/001.png
