A 4-panel bi-fold interior glass partition door with a slim black powder-coated steel frame. Four tall narrow glass leaves hang from a top track; each leaf is split by a horizontal mid-rail into a large upper glass pane and a shorter lower glass pane. The leaves concertina (accordion-fold) in two pairs: the left pair folds flat against the left jamb and the right pair folds flat against the right jamb, meeting in the middle where a slim vertical pull handle sits. Visible hinge knuckles run down the vertical edges between panels and at the jambs.
