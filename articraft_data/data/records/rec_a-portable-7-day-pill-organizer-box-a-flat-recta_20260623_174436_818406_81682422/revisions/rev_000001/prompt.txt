A portable 7-day pill organizer box. A flat rectangular plastic case with seven small compartments in a grid, each numbered 1 to 7 and covered by its own small hinged translucent lid that flips open (revolute). The compartments sit in a colored base tray; a larger clear outer lid closes over the whole box and latches at the front. Pastel translucent colored lids. Proportions ~13 cm wide, ~10 cm deep, ~3 cm tall. Materials: translucent tinted plastic lids, white/colored plastic base, a small front latch. Emit the seven compartment lids with a loop; name parts.
