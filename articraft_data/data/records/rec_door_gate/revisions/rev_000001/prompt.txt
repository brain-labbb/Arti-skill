An ornate wrought-iron double-leaf entrance gate set in an arched stone / plaster surround. Two tall black gate leaves with gold-accented scrollwork and vertical bars swing open from the outer jambs and meet in the middle. Above the gates is a fixed arched fanlight filled with decorative ironwork. Two revolute hinges on vertical axes; the two leaves mirror each other and swing outward. The stone arch and the fanlight are fixed structure. Reference image: picture/Door/Gate/001.png
