Model an MK2-style 'pineapple' fragmentation hand grenade, about 0.115 m tall and 0.06 m in body diameter. The body is an olive-green cast-iron ovoid covered in a deep waffle grid of fragmentation segmentation: roughly 5 horizontal rows of beveled rectangular knobs separated by vertical and horizontal grooves, with a flatter rounded base. A yellow/ochre painted ring band circles the narrow neck where the body meets the fuze. On top sits a polished silver steel fuze assembly: a short threaded collar, a rectangular pivot housing, and a long dark-green curved safety lever (spoon) that hugs down the side of the body. A bright stainless pull ring (torus about 0.03 m diameter) hangs from a cotter-style safety pin inserted horizontally through the fuze housing. Articulations: (1) safety lever, revolute joint at the top pivot lug with horizontal axis, rotating about 90 degrees outward/away from the body as if releasing; (2) pull pin with attached ring, prismatic joint sliding about 0.02 m horizontally along the pin axis to extract from the fuze housing; (3) pull ring, revolute joint about the pin shaft so it can swing/dangle roughly 120 degrees.
