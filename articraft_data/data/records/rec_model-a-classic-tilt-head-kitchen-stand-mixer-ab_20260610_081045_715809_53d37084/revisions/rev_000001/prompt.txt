Model a classic tilt-head kitchen stand mixer, about 0.36 m tall, 0.22 m wide and 0.35 m deep. The cast body is glossy apple-green: a thick C-shaped pedestal base curving up into a rear column, with a horizontal motor head cantilevered forward over the bowl. A chrome band with a white inset stripe wraps the head's nose, and a small sliding speed-control lever sits on the head's right side. A round chrome attachment hub cap sits on the front nose. A glossy white ceramic mixing bowl (about 0.24 m diameter, 0.18 m tall) with a side handle sits on the chromed circular base plate, locked to the pedestal. A polished stainless wire balloon whisk hangs from the underside of the head into the bowl. Articulations: (1) the motor head tilts up via a revolute joint at the rear hinge, axis horizontal left-right, range 0 to about 50 degrees back; (2) the whisk spindle is a continuous rotary joint about its near-vertical axis; (3) the speed lever is a prismatic slider on the head side, travel about 0.04 m front-to-back.
