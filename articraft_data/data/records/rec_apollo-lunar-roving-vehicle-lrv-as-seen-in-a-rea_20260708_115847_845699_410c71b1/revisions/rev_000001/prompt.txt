Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV) as seen in a rear three-quarter photo on the lunar surface: an open flat-deck aluminum chassis riding on four large wire-mesh wheels with dark chevron tread bands, each wheel covered by a broad tan dust fender on struts; a prominent rear equipment rack and tool pallet hangs behind the two webbed seats with backrests, carrying hand-tool handles and sample gear; between the seats sits the control console with a T-handle hand controller; from the forward chassis rises a mast carrying the umbrella-shaped wire-mesh high-gain antenna dish plus a thin low-gain antenna staff and a TV camera unit. Silver-gray chassis and mesh wheels, dark gray tread, tan fenders and webbing, black instrument boxes; the four wheels spin on their axles, the high-gain antenna mast swivels, and the hand controller tilts.
