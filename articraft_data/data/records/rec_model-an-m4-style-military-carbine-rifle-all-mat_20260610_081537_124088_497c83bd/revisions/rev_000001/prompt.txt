Model an M4-style military carbine rifle, all matte black, about 0.85 m long overall, 0.25 m tall including the magazine, and 0.06 m wide. Parts: an upper/lower receiver block with flat-top picatinny rail, a quad-rail (RIS) handguard with ribbed picatinny slots on all four faces, a tapered cylindrical barrel ending in a slotted birdcage flash hider, a triangular fixed front-sight tower, a boxy red-dot reflex sight mounted on the top rail, an angled pistol grip, a curved 30-round box magazine, a ribbed cylindrical vertical foregrip under the handguard, and a skeletonized six-position collapsible buttstock on a cylindrical buffer tube. Articulations: (1) trigger, revolute about a horizontal left-right axis inside the trigger guard, ~25 deg pull; (2) buttstock, prismatic along the buffer tube axis, ~0.09 m of collapse/extend travel; (3) charging handle at the rear of the upper receiver, prismatic rearward along the bore axis, ~0.07 m travel; (4) magazine, prismatic along its own insertion axis (downward-forward), ~0.06 m release travel; (5) safety selector lever on the left receiver side, revolute about a left-right axis, 90 deg throw.
