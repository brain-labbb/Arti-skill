Model a racing-style gaming chair in matte-black PU leather with orange accent piping, an orange diagonal stripe band across the mid backrest, and an orange logo print; includes a black headrest pillow with orange emblem strapped near the top and a black lumbar cushion with orange print resting on the seat back. Overall about 0.7 m wide x 0.7 m deep x 1.3 m tall. The backrest is a tall contoured bucket shape with sculpted side bolsters and shoulder wings; the seat pan has raised side bolsters. Two 4D armrests: black T-shaped pads on vertical stems attached to the seat sides. Below: a tilt mechanism box, chrome gas-lift column, and a polished chrome 5-spoke star base with five black caster wheels. Articulation: (1) continuous swivel of the whole upper chair about the vertical Z gas-lift axis; (2) prismatic gas-lift along Z, 0 to 0.1 m; (3) revolute backrest recline about a horizontal left-right axis at the side hinge plates where backrest meets seat, 0 to -1.2 rad (deep recline); (4) prismatic armrest height adjustment along Z on each stem, 0 to 0.06 m; (5) casters as continuous rolling joints.
