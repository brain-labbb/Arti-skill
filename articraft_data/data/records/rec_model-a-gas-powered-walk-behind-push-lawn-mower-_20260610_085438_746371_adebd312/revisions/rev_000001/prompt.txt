Model a gas-powered walk-behind push lawn mower, about 1.5 m long overall (deck plus raked-back handlebar), 0.55 m wide, handle grip at roughly 1.0 m height. The cutting deck is a glossy burnt-orange/red rounded steel shell, hollow underneath, with a smoothly domed top and a small side skirt; a matte-black single-cylinder engine block with recessed panels, a round recoil-starter cover and a small fuel cap sits centered on the deck. Four matte-black rubber wheels (~0.20 m diameter front, ~0.25 m rear) with gray spoked plastic hubs mount on stub axles at the deck corners. A thin black tubular U-shaped handlebar, with red-accented lower segments and a wire cable running along it, sweeps up and back from the rear of the deck. Articulations: each of the four wheels is a continuous joint spinning about a horizontal lateral (left-right) axis; a flat cutting blade hidden under the deck is a continuous joint about a vertical axis on the engine crankshaft; the handlebar attaches to the rear deck brackets with a revolute folding joint about a horizontal lateral axis, range about 0 to -100 degrees so it folds forward flat over the deck for storage.
