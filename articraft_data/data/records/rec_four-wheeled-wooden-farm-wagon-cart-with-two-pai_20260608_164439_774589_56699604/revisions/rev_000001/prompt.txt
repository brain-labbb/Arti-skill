Four-wheeled wooden farm wagon cart with two pairs of large spoked wooden wheels (smaller steerable front pair, larger rear pair), a weathered plank box body with low side walls, and two long wooden draw poles/shafts extending forward connected by rope. Primary articulation: all four wheels roll (continuous spin) about their axles; the front axle steers (yaws).
