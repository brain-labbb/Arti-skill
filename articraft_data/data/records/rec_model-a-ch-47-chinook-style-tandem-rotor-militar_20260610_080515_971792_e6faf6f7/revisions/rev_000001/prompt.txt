Model a CH-47 Chinook-style tandem-rotor military transport helicopter in matte desert-tan camouflage with darker tan patches, small black windows, and black rotor blades with yellow-green tips. True scale: fuselage about 16 m long, 3.8 m wide, cabin top about 3 m high; overall height to rear rotor hub about 5.7 m; each three-blade rotor spans about 18 m. Parts: a long boxy fuselage with a rounded glazed cockpit nose, a slab-sided cargo cabin with a row of small round windows, side fuel sponsons low on each flank, four fixed landing-gear legs with small black wheels (two per sponson area), a low front pylon above the cockpit and a tall raised rear pylon at the tail, and a rear loading ramp at the upswept tail. Articulations: (1) front rotor, continuous joint spinning about a near-vertical axis tilted slightly forward, three tapered blades on a dark hub; (2) rear rotor, continuous joint on the raised rear pylon, axis near-vertical tilted slightly forward, mounted roughly 2.5 m higher than the front rotor so the disks clear each other; (3) rear cargo ramp, revolute joint on a horizontal lateral axis at the lower tail edge, swinging from closed (flush with tail) down about 60 degrees to ground level.
