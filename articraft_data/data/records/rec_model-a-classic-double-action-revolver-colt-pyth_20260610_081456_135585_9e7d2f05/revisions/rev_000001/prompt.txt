Model a classic double-action revolver (Colt Python style) with a 6-inch barrel, overall about 0.29 m long, 0.14 m tall and 0.04 m wide. Polished stainless steel throughout: a heavy barrel with a full-length underlug shrouding the ejector rod and a vented rib on top with elongated slots, plus front and rear sights. The fluted six-shot cylinder sits in a stainless frame; the grip is warm brown polished walnut with an inset darker panel and a visible grip screw. Parts: frame with barrel, cylinder mounted on a swing-out crane, ejector rod through the cylinder axis, curved trigger inside the open trigger guard, spurred hammer at the rear, and the wood grip. Articulations: (1) crane with cylinder, revolute about an axis parallel to the barrel at the lower front left of the frame, swinging the cylinder out 30-45 degrees to the left; (2) cylinder, continuous rotation about its own longitudinal axis on the crane; (3) trigger, revolute about a left-right pin, ~25 degrees rearward; (4) hammer, revolute about a left-right pin, cocking back ~30 degrees; (5) ejector rod, prismatic along the cylinder axis, ~0.02 m travel.
