Model a spherical playground merry-go-round (orbit spinner): a cage of tubular steel hoops forming a sphere about 1.8 m in diameter, centered on a fixed vertical post. The post is a white-painted square steel column, roughly 0.12 m wide and 2.2 m tall, anchored to the ground and passing through the sphere's vertical axis. The rotating cage consists of: three to four horizontal latitude rings painted sky blue (the largest at the equator, smaller ones above and below), one bright yellow horizontal ring near the lower-middle, and two to three full vertical meridian hoops painted with red-and-white candy stripes that cross at the top and bottom poles. All hoops are round steel tube about 0.04 m in diameter, with a worn, slightly chipped paint look; small rusty bracket clamps join meridians to latitude rings. The hoop cage attaches to upper and lower collar bearings on the post. Articulation: the entire hoop sphere is one rigid child link connected to the static post by a continuous revolute joint about the vertical (Z) axis at the post centerline, spinning freely 360 degrees as children push and ride it.
