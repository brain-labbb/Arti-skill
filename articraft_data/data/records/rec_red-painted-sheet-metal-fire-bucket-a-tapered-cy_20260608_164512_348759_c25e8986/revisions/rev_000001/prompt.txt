Red painted sheet-metal fire bucket: a tapered cylindrical bucket, wider at the open top with a flat bottom, hollow thin-wall weathered red metal body and a rolled top rim. Two riveted pivot lugs on opposite sides of the rim hold a steel-wire bail handle; the handle is a revolute joint pivoting about the horizontal diameter line through the two lugs, swinging from down one side, up over the top, to down the other side.
