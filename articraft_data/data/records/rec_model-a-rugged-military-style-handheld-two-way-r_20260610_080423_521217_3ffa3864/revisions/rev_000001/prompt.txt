Model a rugged military-style handheld two-way radio (walkie-talkie). Main body is a rectangular slab about 0.22 m tall, 0.065 m wide, 0.038 m deep, matte black hard plastic on the upper half and desert-tan on the lower half. The upper face has a recessed green backlit LCD screen with small red and green indicator LEDs beside it; below it sits a tan keypad of about 16 raised rectangular rubber buttons in a 4x4 grid. On the top face: a knurled black rotary volume/channel knob (cylinder ~0.018 m diameter, ~0.015 m tall) and, beside it, a tapered black whip antenna about 0.35 m long with a ribbed base collar. The left side has two oblong rubber push-to-talk (PTT) buttons; the back carries a flat spring belt clip. Articulations: (1) top rotary knob, continuous joint about its vertical axis; (2) PTT button, prismatic joint pressing ~0.003 m inward along the side-normal axis; (3) belt clip, revolute joint at its top mounting screw, hinging ~0-25 degrees away from the back panel; (4) antenna, revolute fold-over joint at its base collar, 0-90 degrees from upright.
