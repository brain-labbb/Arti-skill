Model a modern commercial playground seesaw, about 2.6 m long and 0.9 m tall. The rocker is one thick glossy red-orange steel tube (~120 mm diameter) bent into a smooth shallow banana/U curve, dipping at the center and sweeping up at both ends. At the center underside, a short red stub descends into a matte black cast pivot bracket with visible bolt heads, mounted atop a short light-gray cylindrical ground pedestal (~150 mm diameter). Near each end of the red tube there is a black clamp collar ring with bolts; from each collar a thinner red tube branches downward-outboard to a large flat dark-gray sheet-metal seat (rounded triangular plate with rivets), and another thin red post rises above the tube to a small gray handlebar/grip plate with two hand cutout holes. The two ends mirror each other. Articulation: the entire red curved tube with both seat and handle assemblies rocks on a single revolute joint at the black center bracket, horizontal axis perpendicular to the seesaw length, range about plus/minus 15 degrees.
