Model a square wall-mounted exhaust ventilation fan, about 0.30 m x 0.30 m and 0.06 m deep, in light grey-white plastic. The body is a flat square frame panel with a slightly raised border and a large circular opening. The opening is covered by a concentric-ring safety grille: many thin closely spaced circular rings joined by a few radial spokes, with a solid flat circular disc cap at the center. Directly behind the grille is a multi-blade axial impeller with roughly 7-9 wide, swept/curved blades radiating from a central hub; the blades are visible through the gaps between grille rings. A shallow hollow square housing closes the back. Parts: square frame/housing with integrated ring grille and center cap (fixed) and the impeller. Articulation: one continuous joint — the impeller (hub + curved blades) rotates about the horizontal axis through the center of the panel, normal to the wall, with unlimited rotation. Mount the impeller slightly recessed so blades do not poke through the grille. Reference image: picture/Other/Vent/002.png
