An MSI Gaming Trio style triple-fan gaming graphics card, about 0.32 m long, 0.13 m tall including the metal I/O bracket, and roughly 55 mm (2.5-3 slot) thick, lying flat with its three axial cooling fans facing up. Each of the three fans has many curved black scimitar blades around a glossy black hub badge ringed in red with a small white MSI dragon emblem, and each fan spins freely on its own axis inside a circular shroud opening. The sculpted black plastic shroud has angular gunmetal-gray accent panels between and around the fan openings, a white translucent RGB diffuser strip running along the top edge, and openings that reveal the silver aluminum heatsink fin stack and nickel heatpipes stacked between the shroud and the black PCB below. The black PCB carries a gold PCIe x16 edge connector protruding from its bottom edge, an 8-pin power connector block near the top edge, and a silver slot bracket at the left end with DisplayPort/HDMI port openings and vent slots.
