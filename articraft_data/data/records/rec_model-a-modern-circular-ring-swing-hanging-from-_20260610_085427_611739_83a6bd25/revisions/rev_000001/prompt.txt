Model a modern circular ring swing hanging from a steel pergola frame, about 3.0 m wide and 2.6 m tall overall. The frame has two vertical square-tube galvanized-steel posts (roughly 0.15 m square, 2.4 m tall) with riveted gusset plates at the top corners, supporting a horizontal slatted canopy beam: a row of many thin dark-grey vertical fins/slats (each ~0.04 m thick, 0.25 m deep) spanning between the posts like a louvered pergola roof, capped by a lighter steel top rail. From the canopy centerline a short polished-steel chain (3-4 links plus a hook) suspends the swing: a polished chrome tubular ring about 1.5 m in outer diameter (tube ~0.05 m), actually a twin-ring with a narrow gap, whose lower inner half is lined with warm brown wooden slat planks forming a curved bench seat inside the circle. Articulation: the chain-and-ring assembly swings on a revolute joint at the canopy anchor, axis horizontal and parallel to the beam, range about -45 to +45 degrees (fore-aft swinging). Additionally the ring spins about the vertical chain axis via a continuous joint at the hook. Frame is static. Reference image: picture/Playground/swing circular/001.png
