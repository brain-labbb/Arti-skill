A round cast-iron access hatch / trap door lid, reddish-brown, with a circular spoked relief pattern on top and bolts around the rim, hinged at the rear so it swings upward to open past vertical. It sits on a square metal-mesh collar that is mounted on top of a round concrete / stone well shaft. One revolute joint with a horizontal hinge axis at the rear rim; the collar and the well shaft are fixed. Reference image: picture/Door/Trap door/001.png
