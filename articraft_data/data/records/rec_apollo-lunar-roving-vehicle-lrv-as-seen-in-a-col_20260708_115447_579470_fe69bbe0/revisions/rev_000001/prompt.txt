Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV) as seen in a color side-view photograph on the lunar surface: an open, flat silvery tubular-frame chassis riding on four dark woven wire-mesh wheels, each wheel topped by an orange-tan fender; a tall front mast carries an umbrella-shaped high-gain dish antenna with a central feed spike, and gold-foil-wrapped instrument/communications boxes (LCRU and camera gear) cluster at the front of the chassis; two fold-up tubular-framed seats with white webbing straps sit side by side amidships with a T-handle control console between them; a thin low-gain antenna rod rises behind the console and a rear rack carries equipment/tool boxes over the back wheels. The four wheels spin on their hubs and the dish antenna mast can swivel.
