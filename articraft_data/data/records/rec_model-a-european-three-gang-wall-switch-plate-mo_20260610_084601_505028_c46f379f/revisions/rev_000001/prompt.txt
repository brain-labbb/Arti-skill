Model a European three-gang wall switch plate mounted flat against a wall, as in the reference photo (use the upper panel). Overall faceplate is a horizontal rounded rectangle about 0.24 m wide, 0.09 m tall, protruding roughly 0.012 m from the wall. It has a thin polished-chrome outer trim ring surrounding a matte cream/ivory plastic faceplate. From left to right it carries three square modules (~0.055 m each): two wide rocker light switches and one round-recessed Schuko power socket. Each rocker is a slightly convex square ivory paddle sitting proud of the plate; articulate each as an independent revolute joint about a horizontal axis through the rocker's mid-height (axis parallel to the plate width), with a small see-saw range of about -0.10 to +0.10 rad so pressing the top or bottom tips the paddle. The Schuko module is static: a square ivory frame with a circular recessed well ~0.04 m diameter containing two pin holes and top/bottom grounding clips; model the well as a true hollow recess. Include a small flat wall backing slab in warm beige so the plate reads as wall-mounted. Keep edges softly filleted and the chrome trim as a thin raised border.
