A sectional garage door set in a black steel frame: roughly six to seven horizontal grey panel slats stacked vertically, each slat a flat embossed panel, with a small latch handle near the bottom. It opens by lifting: each slat slides upward and the slats stack and overlap at the top. Model it as a chain of slats - the frame is the base, slat 1 is a child of the frame, slat 2 a child of slat 1, and so on; each prismatic joint travels the same distance straight up (+Z). Slats overlapping at the top in the open pose is intended, not a collision error. Reference image: picture/Door/Garage shutter/001.png
