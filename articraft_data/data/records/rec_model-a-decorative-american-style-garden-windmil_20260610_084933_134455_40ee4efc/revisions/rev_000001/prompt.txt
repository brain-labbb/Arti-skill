Model a decorative American-style garden windmill, about 2.4 m tall with a 0.8 m square base. The body is a tapered four-sided tower clad in horizontal red-painted wooden lapboard siding with white trim: a white-framed hinged door on the lower front, a multi-pane white window above it, and small windows on the side faces. The tower narrows toward the top and is capped by a red pyramidal hip roof with a small square cupola (red walls, dark window openings) and its own tiny pyramidal roof. Near the top of the front face, a short horizontal shaft carries the rotor: five slender white lattice sails (each an open rectangular grid frame, slightly longer than the tower's width, roughly 1.1 m per sail) radiating from a small central hub. Articulations: (1) the sail rotor spins on a continuous joint about the horizontal shaft axis pointing out of the front face; (2) the front door swings open on a revolute hinge about its vertical left edge, range 0 to 100 degrees. Keep the tower hollow-looking with recessed window frames and a slight outward taper from top to base. Reference image: picture/Other/Wildmill/001.png
