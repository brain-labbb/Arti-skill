Model a stylized automated sentry gun turret, about 1.2 m tall with a 1.2 m wide footprint. Four splayed yellow-painted steel legs with dark grey gusset plates and round foot pads support a central column wrapped in perforated yellow mesh, topped by a yellow ring platform and a dark gunmetal hexagonal collar. Above the collar sits a boxy yellow receiver housing with dark recessed top panels, carrying four parallel gun barrels in a 2x2 layout: grey cylindrical barrels passing through short yellow muzzle shrouds with dark end caps. Articulations: (1) yaw joint, continuous revolute about the vertical Z axis between the hexagonal collar and the leg platform, letting the whole gun head spin 360 degrees; (2) pitch joint, revolute about a horizontal axis through side trunnions on the receiver, elevating the four-barrel cluster from -10 to +60 degrees; (3) recoil joint, prismatic along the barrel axis letting the 2x2 barrel cluster slide back about 0.06 m into the receiver. Make legs and barrels hollow tubes, the receiver a hollow box, and keep the perforated mesh as a thin textured cylinder shell. Bright safety-yellow plus dark gunmetal grey color scheme.
