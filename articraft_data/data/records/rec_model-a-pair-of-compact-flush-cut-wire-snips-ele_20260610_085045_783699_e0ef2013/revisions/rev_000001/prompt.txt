Model a pair of compact flush-cut wire snips (electronics side cutters), about 0.13 m long overall, 0.06 m across the splayed handles, and 0.015 m thick. Two mirrored half-tools cross at a single polished stainless-steel rivet: each half is a short tapered cutting jaw (flat, angled blade face meeting at a fine flush-cutting edge) that flows into a slim steel neck, then into a long gently curved handle. The handles are over-molded in glossy bright orange plastic with a lighter translucent peach inlay strip along the top face; jaws and rivet are bare brushed steel. Handles widen and curve outward toward their rounded tips. Articulation: one revolute joint at the central rivet, axis perpendicular to the flat plane of the tool (vertical when the tool lies flat). One half is the base link; the other half rotates roughly 0 to 25 degrees, closing the blade edges together while the handles scissor apart and together in opposition. The two halves must overlap correctly at the pivot boss (one half sits slightly above the other). Keep blades thin and tapered, handles softly rounded with no sharp boxiness.
