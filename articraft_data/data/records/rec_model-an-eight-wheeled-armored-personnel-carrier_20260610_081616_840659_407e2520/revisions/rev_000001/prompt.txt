Model an eight-wheeled armored personnel carrier (BTR-style APC) in olive-drab green, about 7.6 m long, 2.9 m wide, and 2.6 m tall to the turret roof. The hull is a faceted welded box: a steeply sloped, wedge-shaped nose with angled glacis plates, a flat boxy rear troop compartment, and slab sides with fender skirts over the wheel wells. Surface details include stowage boxes, grab rails, side door panels, and a red-and-white tactical marking on the hull side. Four large rubber-tired road wheels per side (eight total, ~1.1 m diameter, dark gray with chunky tread and green hubs) mount low on the hull. On the roof sits a low cylindrical turret with tan/khaki camouflage patches, carrying a long thin autocannon barrel with a muzzle device plus a small coaxial machine gun and a hatch ring. Articulations: (1) turret yaw — continuous revolute about a vertical axis at the turret center; (2) gun elevation — revolute about a horizontal lateral axis at the turret front, range -5 to +35 degrees pitch; (3) each of the 8 wheels — continuous joints about lateral horizontal axles.
