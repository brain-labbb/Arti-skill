A large ESA-style geostationary telecommunications satellite (Artemis-class) deployed in orbit: a dark rectangular bus whose Earth-facing front panel is tiled with dark solar-cell-like radiator plates and dotted with crinkled gold MLI foil equipment patches and instruments near the corners, plus a small dark camera/sensor cylinder; two long deep-blue multi-segment solar array wings extend from opposite sides of the bus on yoke booms driven by solar array drive joints; the dominant identity feature is a pair of large white round dish reflectors, each a shallow saucer with a small feed cutout near the rim, deployed on articulated support arms — one dish swung up over the top-right corner of the bus, the other swung down past the lower-left corner — each dish steerable on its arm gimbal joint.
