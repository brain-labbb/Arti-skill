Model a pair of long slim wolf-jaw blacksmith tongs in dark forged steel with a scratched, semi-matte gray finish. Overall length about 0.55 m, lying flat in one plane. The jaw end, about 0.07 m long, has two short flat jaws that angle slightly toward each other and meet near the tip; the jaw faces carry shallow transverse grooves and the closed jaws form a narrow V notch opening at the very tip for gripping bar stock. Just behind the jaws the two halves cross at a flattened elliptical boss roughly 0.035 m across, joined by a round-head rivet visible on both faces. Two very long, gently splayed reins run about 0.45 m from the boss, tapering continuously from roughly 0.016 m square near the pivot to about 0.007 m round at the tips, with a subtle outward bow. Hammer marks texture the boss and jaw area. Articulation: one revolute joint at the rivet with its axis perpendicular to the tool plane; the moving arm rotates about 0 to 0.3 rad to open the jaws while its rein swings away from the fixed rein.
