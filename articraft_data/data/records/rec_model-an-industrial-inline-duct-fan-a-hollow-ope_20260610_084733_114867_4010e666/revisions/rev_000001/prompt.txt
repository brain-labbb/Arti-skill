Model an industrial inline duct fan: a hollow open metal cylinder about 0.32 m in diameter and 0.28 m long, lying with its axis horizontal, made of brushed silver-grey steel. The cylindrical shell is a simple tube with slightly rolled rims at both ends. The visible front opening is covered by a wire guard made of 3-4 concentric metal rings plus 6-8 radial wire spokes converging on a small central hub plate (spider/target pattern). Inside the tube, just behind the guard, sits an axial impeller: a central cylindrical motor hub with about 6-8 dark curved sheet-metal blades that nearly fill the bore; the motor body extends rearward inside the tube on a strut mount. Parts: cylindrical shell, front wire guard, motor mount (fixed), and the impeller. Articulation: one continuous joint — the impeller spins about the tube's central horizontal axis with unlimited rotation.
