Model a built-in compact microwave oven (Brastemp Gourmand style) designed to mount flush in kitchen cabinetry. Wide landscape proportions: trim frame about 0.60 m wide x 0.45 m tall, body extending ~0.35 m deep as a hollow box. Finish is brushed stainless / light-gray metal on the outer trim frame, with a darker warm-gray (taupe) door panel inset within it. Across the top of the door area runs a fixed control strip with a small digital display, several small push buttons, and four protruding cylindrical metal rotary knobs in a row; a band of short vent slots runs along the top edge of the door. The door occupies most of the front: a darker gray panel with a large wide rectangular frosted-glass window centered in it and a brushed-aluminum bar handle mounted low near the door's bottom edge on two stand-off posts. Articulations: the door is a revolute drop-down joint hinged along its bottom horizontal edge (axis along the width), opening 0 to ~90 degrees from vertical to horizontal; each of the four rotary knobs is a revolute joint about its own horizontal axis perpendicular to the front face (continuous rotation acceptable).
