Model a commercial playground swing set with a glossy red steel A-frame. Overall size about 3.0 m wide, 2.4 m tall, 1.6 m deep at the feet. Each end is an A-shaped pair of square-tube legs joined by two short horizontal cross braces partway down, ending in flat rectangular base plates with bolt holes. A straight red top rail spans between the two A-frames. Two identical swings hang from the rail: each uses two galvanized silver link chains (~1.5 m long, oval links clearly visible) attached to small clevis hangers on the rail, supporting a flexible black rubber belt seat that sags into a shallow U curve, with small red metal clamp plates where the chains meet the seat. Treat each swing (two chains + belt seat) as one rigid pendulum body with the seat modeled in its sagged U shape. Articulation: each swing connects to the top rail via its own revolute joint, axis horizontal along the rail at the hanger points, swinging fore-aft about -1.0 to +1.0 rad, the two swings independent.
