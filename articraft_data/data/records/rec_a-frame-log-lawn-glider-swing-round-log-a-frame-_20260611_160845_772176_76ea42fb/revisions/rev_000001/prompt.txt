A-frame log lawn glider swing: round-log A-frame end supports made of natural unfinished cedar logs, a red pitched gable roof of corrugated panels on top, and a hanging glider unit with two facing contoured log seats and a small center table platform between them. The glider unit hangs from the top beam on four swing arms. Primary articulation: the whole glider unit (both seats plus floor platform) swings as one on revolute hanger joints with mimic links.
