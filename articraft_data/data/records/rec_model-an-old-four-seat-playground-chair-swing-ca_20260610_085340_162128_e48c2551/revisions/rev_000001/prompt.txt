Model an old four-seat playground chair-swing carousel, about 3.2 m in diameter and 1.2 m tall, in heavily weathered cream-white paint with rust stains. A stout rusty orange-red center column (about 0.15 m diameter, 1.0 m tall) carries a rotating hub with a small exposed spindle bolt on top. Four pairs of cream-white steel tubes radiate from the hub: each seat is supported by two tubes that splay apart and cross the neighboring pair, forming a continuous X-lattice truss in plan view. At each of the four corners hangs a slatted sheet-metal seat platform (about 0.5 x 0.4 m, whitish with rust streaks, visible parallel slats) surrounded on three sides by a thin bent-tube guard rail acting as backrest and armrests. Articulation: the whole lattice rotor with its four seats turns on the center column via a continuous joint about the vertical Z axis; each seat hangs from its truss corner on a revolute joint with a horizontal, tangentially oriented axis, swinging about -30 to +30 degrees outward/inward. Keep the structure distinct from brightly painted variants: monochrome cream-white tubes, crossing twin-tube arms, slatted seats with wraparound rails.
