Model heavy-duty combination (lineman) pliers, about 0.20 m long, 0.07 m across the open handles, and 0.025 m thick at the pivot boss. Two crossing forged-steel halves pivot on a large flat circular rivet boss (~0.025 m diameter, brushed silver with a visible circular seam). The jaw end is polished steel: a blunt squared nose with fine horizontal gripping serrations on the inner faces, a rounded serrated pipe-grip recess behind the nose, and short wire-cutter edges near the pivot. Behind the pivot, each handle is over-molded with a thick soft-touch grip: matte black rubber body with a broad glossy red inlay running along the outer/top face, flared thumb guards near the pivot, and bulbous rounded handle ends. Handles curve gently outward. Articulation: one revolute joint at the rivet, axis perpendicular to the flat tool plane. One half is fixed; the other rotates about 0 to 30 degrees, opening the serrated jaws as the handles spread. The halves must interleave at the pivot (one passes over the other) with the rivet boss visible on the outer face. Reference image: picture/Other/pliers/002.png
