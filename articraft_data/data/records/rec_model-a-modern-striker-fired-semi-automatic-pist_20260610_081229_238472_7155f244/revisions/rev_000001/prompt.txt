Model a modern striker-fired semi-automatic pistol (P320-style), about 0.21 m long, 0.15 m tall and 0.034 m wide. Two-tone finish: matte black steel slide with angled front and rear cocking serrations and a rear sight, over an olive-drab/gray polymer frame with black stippled grip panels, an undercut trigger guard, and a Picatinny accessory rail with cross slots under the dust cover. An extended magazine with a black baseplate protrudes slightly below the grip. Parts: frame with grip, slide with barrel/ejection port, curved black trigger, takedown lever on the left side above the trigger, and a detachable box magazine. Articulations: (1) slide, prismatic along the barrel axis, retracting rearward about 0.045 m and returning; (2) trigger, revolute about a horizontal left-right pin at its top, rotating rearward about 25 degrees; (3) magazine, prismatic along the grip axis (tilted slightly rearward of vertical), dropping about 0.10 m out of the magwell; (4) takedown lever, revolute about a left-right axis, rotating 90 degrees.
