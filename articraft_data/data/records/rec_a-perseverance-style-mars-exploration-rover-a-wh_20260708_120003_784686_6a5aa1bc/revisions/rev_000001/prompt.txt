A Perseverance-style Mars exploration rover: a white boxy chassis body with a flat equipment deck carrying instrument boxes and orange accents, six black cleated wheels on a gray rocker-bogie suspension with steering knuckles on the corner wheels, a vertical gray camera mast near the front topped by a white box-shaped stereo camera head with dark lenses, a multi-jointed robotic arm mounted on the front-right ending in a dark cylindrical instrument turret with a drill, a round gray high-gain antenna dish on a gimbal pedestal on the deck, and a small dark cylindrical UHF antenna on a pole at the rear of the deck. The six wheels spin, the camera mast yaws and its head tilts, the arm swings at its shoulder, and the antenna dish rotates on its pedestal.
