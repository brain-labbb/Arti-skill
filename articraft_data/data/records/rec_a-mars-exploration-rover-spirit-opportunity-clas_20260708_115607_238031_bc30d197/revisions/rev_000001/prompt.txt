A Mars Exploration Rover (Spirit/Opportunity class) solar-powered rover: a low box-shaped warm-gray electronics body (WEB) topped by a large flat deck of dark blue-black solar panels in a winged hexagonal layout with visible cell grid lines, two rear wing panels angled slightly, a tall pancam mast rising from the front-center of the deck with a pan/tilt stereo camera head bar holding two camera eyes, a low-gain antenna whip and a high-gain antenna dish on the deck, six small dark cleated wheels on silver rocker-bogie suspension arms (front and rear steering knuckles), and a jointed instrument arm tucked under the front of the body ending in an instrument turret. Articulated: all six wheels spin, the mast head pans, the head camera bar tilts, and the front instrument arm shoulder pivots.
