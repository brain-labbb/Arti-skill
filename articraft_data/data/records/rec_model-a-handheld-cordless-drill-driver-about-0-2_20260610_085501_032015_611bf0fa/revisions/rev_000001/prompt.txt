Model a handheld cordless drill/driver, about 0.22 m long, 0.20 m tall and 0.07 m wide. The body is bright yellow-green molded plastic with black rubberized overmold panels along the motor housing top, pistol grip and battery; small vent slots perforate the housing sides. At the front sits a black keyless chuck assembly: a knurled chuck sleeve with three exposed steel jaw tips, behind it a ribbed torque-adjustment clutch collar with numbered settings. The pistol grip descends at a slight rake to a large black slide-on battery pack labeled 18V 1.3Ah LITHIUM-ION in yellow text. Articulations: (1) chuck/spindle, continuous joint spinning about the horizontal drill axis (X); (2) clutch collar, revolute about the same axis, roughly +/-60 deg between torque settings; (3) trigger, revolute about a horizontal axis (Y) at its top edge, squeezing about 15 deg into the grip; (4) forward/reverse selector button, prismatic through the housing above the trigger, +/-0.006 m left-right; (5) battery pack, prismatic joint sliding about 0.05 m rearward along the foot rail to detach.
