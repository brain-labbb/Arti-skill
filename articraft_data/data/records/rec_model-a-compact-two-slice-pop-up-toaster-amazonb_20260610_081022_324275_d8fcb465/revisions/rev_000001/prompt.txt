Model a compact two-slice pop-up toaster (AmazonBasics style), about 0.28 m long, 0.16 m wide, 0.19 m tall, standing on four short dark plastic feet. The body is a single rounded-edge shell in matte medium-gray plastic with two parallel bread slots in the top, each slot framed by a recessed lighter-gray rim. The front face carries a flat brushed-silver control panel. On the panel: a vertical slot with a gray carriage lever knob — model this as a prismatic joint sliding vertically downward about 0.07 m (down = lowering bread, spring-returns up); this is the primary articulation and should also raise/lower an internal bread carriage visible through the top slots if convenient. Below-left of the lever are three small round silver push buttons labeled CANCEL, FROZEN, BAGEL — each a tiny prismatic press of ~0.003 m into the panel. At the bottom-right of the panel is a round dark-gray browning dial with markings 1-4 — a revolute joint about a horizontal axis perpendicular to the front face, rotating roughly 270 degrees. Keep the shell hollow at the slots; use smooth filleted box forms. Reference image: picture/Kitchen/Toaster/001.png
