Classic Mercedes 300SL Gullwing sports car in deep maroon red: long curved lofted lower body and rounded greenhouse cabin (not a box), four chrome-rimmed wheels with rubber tires that roll, tinted windshield and side windows, round chrome headlights, chrome grille and bumpers, side fender vents. Primary articulation: the front driver door opens on a vertical hinge at its front edge (revolute); the four wheels also spin (continuous).
