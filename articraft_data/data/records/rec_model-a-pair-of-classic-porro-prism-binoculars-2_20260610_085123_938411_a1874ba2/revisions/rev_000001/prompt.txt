Model a pair of classic Porro-prism binoculars, 20x50 style, about 0.20 m long, 0.19 m wide (barrels fully open) and 0.06 m tall. Material: matte black pebble-textured rubber armor over metal bodies, with dark gray metal hinge bridge and eyepiece rings; objective lenses show a faint amber/red anti-reflective tint inside black rims. Structure: two mirrored offset barrels, each a wide tapered objective tube (about 0.06 m diameter) stepping inward to a narrower prism housing and a slim eyepiece tube ending in a soft rubber eyecup. The barrels connect through a central hinge bridge carrying a knurled center focus wheel. Articulations: (1) central hinge, revolute about the longitudinal optical-bridge axis, letting the two barrels fold toward each other for interpupillary adjustment, roughly +/- 25 degrees per barrel from the open pose; (2) knurled center focus wheel, continuous rotation about the same central longitudinal axis; (3) right eyepiece diopter ring, revolute about that eyepiece's own viewing axis, about +/- 60 degrees.
