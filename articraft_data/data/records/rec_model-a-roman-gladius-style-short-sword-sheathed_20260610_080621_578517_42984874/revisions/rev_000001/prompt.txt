Model a Roman gladius-style short sword sheathed in an ornate scabbard, about 0.75 m long overall and 0.06 m wide at the scabbard mouth. The sword has a straight double-edged steel blade (~0.45 m, grey pattern-welded finish), a rectangular brass guard block with embossed relief panels, an amber-orange polished wooden grip whose middle section is a twisted gold spiral collar, and a rounded amber-orange pommel capped by a small brass finial. The scabbard is warm reddish-brown burl wood or leather with lighter tan straps crossing in two X patterns along its length, a wide embossed brass throat fitting at the mouth, an engraved tapering brass chape at the tip ending in a small ball, and two small brass suspension rings near the throat. Articulation: (1) the sword is a prismatic joint sliding along the scabbard's long axis, drawing the blade fully out over a ~0.5 m travel from fully sheathed to fully drawn; (2) each of the two brass suspension rings is a revolute joint swinging about its mounting lug axis (perpendicular to the scabbard length), range about -60 to +60 degrees.
