An automatic self-adjusting wire stripper with red-and-black soft-grip handles. Black stamped-steel flat body with a large chrome central pivot; the head carries two opposing black jaw blocks - a gripping jaw and a stripping jaw - plus a brass tension adjustment screw, a small red plastic wire-length stop slider and its coil spring. A serrated wire-cutter notch section sits between the pivot and the grips. Squeezing the handles pivots the arms so the jaws grip and strip the wire.
