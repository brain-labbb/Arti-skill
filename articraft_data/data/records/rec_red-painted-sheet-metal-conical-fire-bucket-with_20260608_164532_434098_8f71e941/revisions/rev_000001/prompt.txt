Red painted sheet-metal conical fire bucket with a pointed bottom (apex down) so it cannot stand upright: hollow thin-wall weathered red metal cone, open circular top with a rolled rim. Two riveted pivot lugs on opposite sides of the rim hold a steel-wire bail handle; the handle is a revolute joint pivoting about the horizontal diameter line through the two lugs, swinging from down one side, up over the top, to down the other side.
