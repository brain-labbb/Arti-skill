Model a WWII-era single-engine fighter airplane (Bearcat/Mustang style) in glossy medium blue with white "402" markings on the fuselage and wings and a small yellow "B" on the tail fin. Overall length about 8.5 m, wingspan about 10.5 m. Parts: a tapered cylindrical fuselage with a rounded radial-engine cowling at the nose and a slim tail cone; a low-mounted straight wing with rounded tips; horizontal stabilizers and a swept vertical fin with yellow tip; a small teardrop canopy bump on the fuselage spine; a polished blue spinner hub at the nose carrying four slender black propeller blades with yellow tips, blades slightly twisted and tapered. Articulation: (1) the propeller assembly (spinner plus all four blades as one link) spins on a continuous joint about the fuselage longitudinal axis at the nose; (2) the rudder is a revolute flap on the trailing edge of the vertical fin, hinge axis vertical, range about -30 to +30 degrees; (3) the elevator spans the trailing edges of both horizontal stabilizers as one link, revolute about a lateral horizontal axis, range about -25 to +25 degrees. Keep blades visibly twisted airfoil slabs, not flat rectangles. Reference image: picture/Military/Aircraft/001.png
