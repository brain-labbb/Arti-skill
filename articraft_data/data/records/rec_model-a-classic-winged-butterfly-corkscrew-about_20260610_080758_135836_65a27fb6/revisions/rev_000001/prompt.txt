Model a classic winged (butterfly) corkscrew, about 0.17 m tall, 0.05 m wide at the body and 0.16 m wide tip-to-tip with both wings raised. The central body is matte black metal: a flat vertical frame with two rivet bosses near the top and a hollow bell-shaped skirt at the bottom (open underneath, tapering outward) that seats on the bottle neck. Through the body runs a polished chrome shaft ending in a chrome T-bar handle on top; below the body the shaft becomes a helical worm screw (model the helix as a swept coil or stacked tilted tori) visible inside the bell. Two chrome lever wings, each a flat tapered arm with a forked spade tip, pivot on the side rivets; their inner ends carry small toothed sectors that mesh with a rack on the shaft. Articulations: left wing, revolute at its rivet about a horizontal axis perpendicular to the body plane, range 0 to about 100 degrees (raises as the screw drives in); right wing, mirrored revolute with the same range; T-handle plus worm, continuous revolute about the vertical shaft axis, optionally paired with a prismatic vertical travel of about 0.04 m through the body.
