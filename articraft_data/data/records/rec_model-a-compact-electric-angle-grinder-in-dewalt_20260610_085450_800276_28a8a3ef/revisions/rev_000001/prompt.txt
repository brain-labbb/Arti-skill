Model a compact electric angle grinder in DeWalt styling: bright yellow glass-filled nylon body with black accents, about 0.28 m long overall with a 0.115 m diameter abrasive disc. The main body is a roughly cylindrical motor housing (~0.07 m diameter) that tapers slightly toward a rear grip, with recessed black ventilation slots, a black rectangular brand label panel, and a black rubber power cord exiting the rear. At the front, the housing transitions into a yellow right-angle gear head with a threaded side-handle boss on its flank; the spindle exits the gear head perpendicular to the motor axis. Mounted on the spindle is a thin grey-brown fiber sanding/grinding disc held by a small hex clamp flange nut. Articulations: (1) the disc-and-spindle assembly is a continuous joint spinning about the spindle axis (perpendicular to the motor axis), unlimited rotation; (2) a black paddle-style slide power switch on top of the motor housing is a prismatic joint translating about 0.012 m along the motor axis between OFF (rear) and ON (forward); (3) a small round spindle-lock button on the gear head is a prismatic joint pressing 0.004 m inward along the spindle axis.
