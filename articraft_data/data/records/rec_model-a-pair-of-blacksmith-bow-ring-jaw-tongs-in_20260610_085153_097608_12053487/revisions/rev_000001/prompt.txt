Model a pair of blacksmith bow (ring-jaw) tongs in weathered dark gray forged steel with a hammered, slightly mottled surface. Overall length about 0.5 m, lying in a single plane roughly 0.04 m thick at the boss. The distinctive jaw end is a large open oval ring about 0.12 m tall and 0.09 m wide, formed by the two curved jaw halves meeting at a small flat tip at the very top; each half-ring is a flat bar with rectangular cross-section. Below the ring the two halves cross at a riveted boss: a flattened circular hinge plate roughly 0.04 m in diameter with a visible round rivet head at its center. From the boss, two long slender reins extend downward about 0.38 m, tapering from roughly 0.018 m square near the boss to about 0.008 m at the rounded ends, splaying apart at a narrow angle. Articulation: one revolute joint at the rivet, axis perpendicular to the tool plane, letting one handle-plus-half-ring arm swing relative to the other; range about 0 to 0.4 rad so the ring jaws open and close like scissors.
