Rooftop yagi/TV antenna on a tall vertical mast: a weathered metal mast standing on the roof at z=0 with two small standoff brackets, topped by a rotatable antenna head consisting of a horizontal yagi boom carrying graduated dipole rod elements, two small junction/balun boxes, and a wide flat reflector grid of many parallel thin rods at the rear; primary articulation is azimuth rotation of the whole head about the vertical mast axis, plus a secondary elevation tilt of the boom.
